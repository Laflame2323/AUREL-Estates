# AUREL Estates — revised design brief

- **Visual reference:** User-supplied AUREL Estates image: a contemporary glass-and-stone residence, landscaped grounds, pool, warm interior lighting, and a deep blue dusk sky.
- **Direction:** Authentic architectural editorial. Photography leads; typography and interface stay quiet and confident.
- **Palette:** Deep navy `#102B4B`, warm architectural ivory `#F2F0EB`, muted stone `#E7E9E8`, restrained champagne gold `#D8B75F`.
- **Typography:** Georgia editorial display paired with clean sans-serif navigation and interface labels.
- **Hero:** Full-bleed user-provided architectural photograph, existing AUREL wordmark embedded in the image, restrained navigation, strong white serif headline.
- **Property collection:** Photographic property cards with understated metadata and working category filters/details.
- **Approach feature:** Large architectural photograph paired with concise editorial copy.
- **Other sections:** Light ivory editorial story, pale stone collection, deep navy enquiry section and footer.
- **Motion:** restrained fades and image hover transitions; no excessive animation.
- **Honesty:** AUREL and property details remain fictional portfolio concepts. Contact form remains a front-end demo until connected to a real inbox/CRM.
